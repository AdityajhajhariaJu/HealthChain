## International privacy supplement
**EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** where those laws apply, ordinary account/service processing can rely on performance of the requested contract; proportionate security processing on legitimate interests; and required records on a legal obligation. Consent applies to optional measurement and to sensitive health processing where required. Special-category health processing requires a valid additional legal basis, commonly explicit consent for this consumer service; contract performance alone is not that additional basis.

You may request access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable conditions. A GDPR rights request is generally answered within one month; legally permitted extensions require notice. You can complain to your supervisory authority without first resolving a complaint with HealthChain. International transfers require an applicable lawful mechanism. If an EU/UK representative or other contact is legally required for the launch scope, that appointment and contact information must be finalized before distribution there.

**India:** privacy/grievance requests go to the contact in this notice. Rights and duties under India's applicable privacy/data-protection rules apply according to their scope and commencement provisions. We do not claim that publication of this notice completes every obligation under the phased Digital Personal Data Protection framework. Any required grievance contact, notice, consent and child-data safeguards must match the deployed service.

**Canada, Australia, Brazil and other markets:** applicable rights can include access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, an explanation of processing, portability or a complaint to the relevant privacy authority. Mandatory consumer and privacy rights remain available where the law applies. Contact us to identify the applicable request and authority; this paragraph does not replace a country-specific requirement that the operator must implement.

We do not infer sensitive-data consent from a generic “International” tab or waive mandatory rights by asking you to use English. Request assistance or an accessible notice if needed. Service availability must be limited to markets for which the operator's required contacts, transfer safeguards and service obligations have been completed.
