## International privacy supplement
These additions apply where the relevant law covers the operator and processing. India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, Switzerland, Italy and Brazil are all primary audiences. Other countries are also intended markets, subject to applicable law and store/provider availability. International availability is not a claim of approval in every country.

### European Union and European Economic Area, including Germany and Italy
Ordinary account/service processing can rely on performance of the requested contract; proportionate security on legitimate interests; and required legal records on legal obligations. Health information has additional protection: this consumer service asks for **explicit consent** to cloud health processing and a separate choice for AI. Contract performance alone is not the additional health-data basis. Optional measurement also requires its own choice.

Where applicable, rights include access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection and consent withdrawal. We ordinarily answer a GDPR rights request within one month; a permitted extension requires a timely explanation. You may complain directly to your local supervisory authority, including the relevant German state authority or Italy's [Garante](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/). You do not need to complain to us first.

The account database is in India; Google, hosting and support processing can occur elsewhere. Applicable transfers need an adequacy decision or another lawful safeguard such as appropriate standard contractual clauses and the required assessment. An in-app checkbox alone is not a substitute for those safeguards. Required representative/contact arrangements must be established for the actual operator and distribution scope before distribution there.

### United Kingdom
UK health data also needs an additional special-category condition, commonly explicit consent for this consumer service. The separate health/AI choices and applicable access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal rights described above are available. The ordinary UK GDPR request period is one month, subject to lawful extension. You can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

UK overseas processing needs the applicable safeguard, such as adequacy, an International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to appropriate contractual clauses with the required assessment. EU clauses alone do not automatically settle UK requirements. Any required UK representative/contact must match the actual operator and service scope.

### Switzerland
Health information is sensitive under Swiss data-protection law. Where processing relies on consent, sensitive-data consent must be explicit. You may request access, correction and other applicable remedies and complain to the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en). Overseas processing must meet the applicable destination and safeguard rules. A Swiss representative is required only when the statutory conditions for the foreign operator's processing are met; it is not automatically required for every foreign app.

### Australia
Health information is sensitive personal information. Providing a health service and holding health information can bring an organization within the Australian Privacy Act even when it is a small business; the operator's Australian connection and actual service determine applicability. Where applicable, the Australian Privacy Principles govern consent/collection, notice, security, access, correction and overseas handling. Contact the privacy inbox to request access or correction, withdraw consent or complain. You can also complain to the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints).

Our India database and other overseas provider locations are disclosed above. Applicable overseas protections depend on the actual provider contract and control of information. We do not describe every overseas cloud operation as the same kind of legal disclosure or use a blanket consent to waive the operator's obligations.

### Brazil
Health information is sensitive personal data under the LGPD. Where consent is the basis, it must be specific and highlighted for the stated purposes. Contact the privacy inbox for applicable confirmation, access, correction, anonymization/blocking/deletion, portability, sharing information, consent withdrawal and explanation of refusal. You can complain to the [Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br).

International handling must use a valid Brazilian transfer mechanism where required. India's database location is not made adequate merely by the EU–Brazil adequacy arrangements. Other appropriate mechanisms can include ANPD-approved contractual clauses. A request for an accessible notice or language assistance does not waive your rights.
