## United States privacy supplement
Where an applicable US state privacy law covers the operator and your information, you may have rights to confirm processing, access/correct/delete data, obtain a portable copy, and appeal a refusal. An authorized agent can submit a request under the law's verification rules. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use sensitive health information for targeted ads. Optional measurement is not required for access. A Global Privacy Control signal is respected as a refusal of optional measurement.

For California and other covered state requests, contact the privacy email in this notice. Account, health/content, activity/device, technical, payment and derived-information categories and their purposes/recipients are described above. We do not offer a financial incentive conditioned on selling your information. Additional legally required limits on sensitive-data use apply.

Consumer health privacy can apply even where HIPAA does not. Read the separate [Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice](/consumer-health-privacy) for health categories, recipients, withdrawal, deletion and appeals, including applicable Washington/Nevada rights. HealthChain is a consumer information-organizing service and does not present this notice as a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices for a clinical provider. If an applicable health-data incident creates notification duties, the operator must follow those duties; a medical disclaimer does not remove them.

Response deadlines, extensions, exemptions and complaint rights follow the applicable state law. We will explain an applicable refusal and appeal route. This supplement does not claim that every state statute applies regardless of its scope or thresholds.
