## Agreements and transfers

A separate paid legal reviewer or bespoke signed DPA is not automatically required for every provider. Gemini's paid terms incorporate Google's processor terms; Supabase's current DPA forms part of its agreement. Verify the actual operator/account agreement and version rather than inventing signatures or treating a pricing screenshot as acceptance evidence. Supabase's DPA permits sensitive data but requires a separate BAA for HIPAA PHI. Its selected database region does not fix every support/subprocessor processing location.

Vercel's standard DPA applies within its stated Pro/Enterprise scope and excludes sensitive data. A paid self-service Pro BAA is available for eligible HIPAA workloads; that does not establish that HealthChain has one or that a BAA covers every intended market/data path.
