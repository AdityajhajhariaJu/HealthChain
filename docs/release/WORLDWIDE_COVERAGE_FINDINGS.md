# Worldwide coverage findings — 6 October 2026

India, USA, UK, Germany, Australia, Switzerland, Italy and Brazil are all primary markets. Worldwide remains the distribution intent. This review preserves the current record-organization, explanation and clinician-preparation service, its 18+ audience, existing providers and mobile-only monthly subscriptions.

## What was checked

The [country and territory inventory](WORLDWIDE_MARKET_INVENTORY.csv) contains **251 entries**: current ordinary two-letter IANA regions, plus Kosovo. It includes territories and Sark; it is not a count of sovereign countries. Each entry has a separately recorded Gemini, Apple and Google result. The [UNCTAD Global Cyberlaw Tracker](https://unctad.org/topic/ecommerce-and-digital-economy/ecommerce-law-reform/summary-adoption-e-commerce-legislation-worldwide), updated 9 September 2026, supplies a **195-country** privacy, consumer and cybercrime legislation screen. Its privacy column records 155 with legislation, 5 with drafts, 33 without legislation and 2 without data. The published [data file](https://storage.unctad.org/2026-cyberlaw_tracker/assets/data/data.csv) was joined without missing or duplicate country matches.

The tracker records whether legislation exists. It does not establish commencement, foreign-business applicability, health-data permission, local representatives or compliance by HealthChain. The national checks below examine those issues in additional markets, alongside the [primary-market review](WORLDWIDE_PRIVACY_REVIEW.md). Rows marked `legislation-inventory-only` have not received that additional national analysis. Territory rows without a UN country record are explicitly unresolved; they do not automatically inherit the parent country's legal or provider result. `not-attested` means no compliance certification was made, rather than an instruction to block every market.

The architecture assessed is local device records with optional Supabase synchronization in Mumbai; selected health context sent through Vercel to paid Gemini with separate permission; optional device imports; user exports; and native store billing. Operator identity/establishment, actual scale, provider contracts and some retention facts are still unknown. Applicability conclusions that need those facts remain conditional. This report identifies relevant launch questions; it does not appoint contacts, file permits or select store territories.

## How to finish the contacts and factual checks

A general privacy inbox, a named grievance/privacy officer, a DPO and a locally established representative are different roles. A public contact address does not create a legal appointment. A DPO can sometimes be internal, but must satisfy the relevant competence, accessibility and independence/conflict requirements; the person who decides all processing purposes cannot automatically serve as an independent GDPR DPO. A representative can cover multiple relevant countries where the law permits, but must actually be established in the required jurisdiction and mandated for the role. No paid contact package was selected.
