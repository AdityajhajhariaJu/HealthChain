The [current pricing page](https://www.flowmusic.app/pricing) lists Plus, but its readable comparison does not independently state the complete commercial licence conditions.

Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) say that Google does not claim ownership of original generated content and require respect for other people's rights and applicable additional terms. These sources support a commercial-use basis for eligible creations. They do not establish that a current subscription retroactively licenses earlier generations or that every exported file used only material the operator was entitled to use.

Commercial-use permission and exclusive copyright are separate. The [U.S. Copyright Office's AI report announcement](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html) explains that copyright requires sufficient human expression; prompts alone do not establish it. Preserve human-written lyrics, recordings, arrangements and creative edits where relevant. Do not describe this preparation as registration, exclusive worldwide copyright or a third-party non-infringement guarantee.

Flow Music's [song guide](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17084348?hl=en) uses Cover as an editing/remix option for a user's own song. A Cover label in the screenshots is therefore not evidence that someone else's composition was used. If a particular song actually uses another person's composition, recording, sample, lyrics or voice, retain the appropriate permission for that input as well.
