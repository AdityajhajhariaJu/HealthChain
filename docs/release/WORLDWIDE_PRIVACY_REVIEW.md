# Worldwide privacy review — 6 October 2026

The requested audiences are worldwide. India, USA, UK, Germany, Australia, Switzerland, Italy and Brazil have equal primary status. This review covers those markets and the common EU/EEA framework. The [expanded country findings](WORLDWIDE_COVERAGE_FINDINGS.md) and [251-entry inventory](WORLDWIDE_MARKET_INVENTORY.csv) now add provider/store checks, a 195-country official legislation screen and additional national health/contact/transfer findings. Review depth is explicit per entry; countries screened only for legislation existence are not described as legally cleared. No market has been silently removed and no new geographic restriction has been enabled.

## Completed technical and notice work

This follow-up adds explicit cloud health permission, distinct from terms, AI, measurement and device permissions; a usable local-only choice; account/version-specific receipts; withdrawal; cancellation/discard of late cloud responses; and preservation of queued local changes. The receipt stays on the device and is not a central legal audit trail. Account-wide requests use the monitored privacy contact and administrative procedure. Older clients are unchanged until updated.

The separate Consumer Health Privacy Notice now has a direct homepage link. Privacy notices identify Supabase, Vercel and paid Gemini; sensitive input categories; India database storage and overseas processing; paid Gemini's 55-day abuse-monitoring retention; deletion limits; local-storage risks; and rights/contact routes. The app does not claim a separately encrypted local vault, HIPAA certification or universal medical-device approval. Public policies remain drafts because the legal operator and some account/retention arrangements are not supplied.
