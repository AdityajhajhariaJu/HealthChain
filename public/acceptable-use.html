Acceptable Use

Updated 2026-10-04 · International

1. Purpose and scope

These rules apply to HealthChain accounts, uploaded documents/images, prompts, support messages, generated content, research links and use of service infrastructure. They protect private health records, service availability and lawful use. They do not turn HealthChain into a medical provider or authorize a use that the Terms prohibit.

2. Records, consent and privacy

Submit only information you have a right to process. Do not upload another person's medical files, identity information, payment details or private messages without appropriate authority. Do not impersonate a clinician, another user, a research team or HealthChain support. Do not attempt to access, export, alter or delete another account's information.

Do not use the app to identify, stalk, discriminate against or threaten a person because of their health, disability or other protected characteristics. Do not publish an exported private record without a lawful basis and the required permission.

3. Health and AI misuse

Do not represent AI output as a verified diagnosis, prescription or review by a licensed clinician. Do not use the app to replace emergency care, make a regulated clinical decision without appropriate authorization, falsify a clinical record, or produce a misleading medical credential.

Do not prompt the service to facilitate self-harm, violence, exploitation, unlawful drug distribution or dangerous medical instructions. A safety refusal or a suggestion to seek professional help is not a diagnosis. Do not try to evade safeguards by hiding instructions in a document or image.

4. Illegal or abusive material

Do not submit content involving child sexual abuse, unlawful exploitation, credible violent threats, hate-based abuse, fraud, malware or infringement of another person's rights. Do not harass support staff or other people. Do not use the service for deceptive marketing, fabricated testimonials or unlawful claims about health products.

5. Service integrity

Do not automate high-volume requests without permission, rotate accounts to evade quotas, forge purchase proofs, share authentication tokens, probe private records, bypass account controls, or disrupt providers. Ordinary use of an available export is permitted; bulk harvesting other users' information is not.

Report a suspected vulnerability to healthchain360@gmail.com with reproducible information that does not include another person's records. Stop once a privacy risk is demonstrated. Do not continue exploitation or publish secrets.

6. Reporting, action and appeal

Send an abuse or infringement report to healthchain360@gmail.com with the relevant location, a description, your contact details and the basis for the report. Do not include unnecessary medical data. If there is an immediate danger, contact appropriate emergency or law-enforcement services.

We may reject a request, remove unlawful material from systems we control, restrict access, preserve limited evidence where legally appropriate, or refer a legally required matter to authorities. We will use proportionate action and provide notice or an appeal route where practical and required. Email support to challenge a restriction and explain the circumstances.

Privacy requests and mandatory consumer rights remain available during a restriction. We do not promise continuous human monitoring of private records or AI conversations.
