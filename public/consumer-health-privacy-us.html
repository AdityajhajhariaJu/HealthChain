Consumer Health Privacy

Updated 2026-10-05 · United States

1. Consumer health data we collect

This separate consumer health notice applies to HealthChain's handling of information that identifies a person's health status under applicable consumer health privacy laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar Nevada protections where applicable. The regulated operator is [Operator name awaiting confirmation], [Correspondence address awaiting confirmation]. Requests: healthchain360@gmail.com.

Categories include symptoms and conditions you report; medication/allergy/history information; uploaded health records/images; health measurements; diet, sleep, activity, hydration and wellbeing logs; permitted device-imported steps, sleep, heart rate and calories; records of seeking health information within your private case; and AI-generated summaries, observations and inferences about those records. Account identifiers can link those categories to you.

HealthChain does not collect precise geolocation to identify visits to healthcare facilities and does not use a geofence for health-based targeting. A country/city you intentionally enter for a trial search is distinct from continuous location tracking.

2. Sources and purposes

Sources are you, documents/photos you choose to provide, permitted Apple Health/Health Connect records, account records and derived outputs from the requested features. We use the information to organize and synchronize your workspace, display your permitted records, generate AI outputs you authorize, provide logs/reminders you select, and carry out necessary security/support functions.

We obtain consent where required unless processing is necessary to provide a product or service you requested and the applicable law permits that exception. Device access and third-party AI processing require their separate choices. Optional product measurement excludes the health details listed above. We do not sell consumer health data or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Categories shared and recipients

For the purposes above, relevant stored records and identifiers are processed by Supabase; API inputs and connection information by Vercel; and the data needed for a selected AI feature by Google Gemini API after permission. Permitted device samples come from Apple Health or Google Health Connect at your direction. You can export records or open external links yourself.

The categories potentially disclosed for requested AI processing include relevant symptoms/history, messages, case/Health Memory context, meal information and chosen documents/images. Hosting/database processors handle the records required for storage, delivery and security. We may make a disclosure required by law or needed to address an actual safety/security issue where the law permits it.

The identified recipients are Supabase, Vercel and Google for those processing functions; Apple/Google device platforms supply permissions and health imports. Affiliates receiving private health records: none identified in the current service configuration. Payment services receive transaction details rather than the contents of a health case.

A new category of consumer health data or a new sharing purpose requires an updated disclosure and any legally required affirmative permission before that new processing begins. A policy update alone does not provide that permission.

4. Access, withdrawal and deletion

You can request access to consumer health data, ask which disclosures/recipients apply to you, withdraw applicable collection/sharing permission and request deletion by contacting healthchain360@gmail.com. The app also provides export, individual-record controls, AI withdrawal and Settings → Delete Account. Where law requires, deletion requests will be communicated to applicable processors and recipients and include the treatment of backups required by that law.

We verify requests proportionately. An authorized agent can submit a request with appropriate proof of authority. We will respond within the applicable legal period, explain any permitted extension or refusal, and identify an available appeal process. You can appeal by emailing the same contact with “Privacy appeal” in the subject. Washington residents can complain to the Washington Attorney General; other residents can use their relevant authority.

Withdrawal can make the health service you asked for unavailable, but does not remove unrelated mandatory rights or retrospectively recall already-completed processing. For a cloud account, account deletion is the complete in-app route to end active health-record storage. Exports under your control are outside the operator's deletion tools.
